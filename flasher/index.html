    Home Assistant (optional)
    
Give the wall an MQTT username and password from
    Home Assistant (a dedicated user under Settings → People works) and it
    finds the broker on its own — Home Assistant advertises itself on the
    LAN — then appears as a light: brightness slider, scenes, automations,
    no HA YAML. Leave both empty to keep this off.

    
    
    
    
    

    Manual broker (advanced)
    
Only needed when the broker is not the Home
    Assistant Mosquitto add-on. A host here skips the auto-discovery.

    
    
    
    
    

    

    
The Home Assistant slider is always 0–100%. A
    floor and ceiling squeeze what the panel actually does: the slider's 0%
    dims only to the floor and its 100% reaches only the ceiling — keep the
    wall from going fully dark at night or blazing bright at noon. Leave
    both empty for the full range.
